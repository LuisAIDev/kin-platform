| kin.health.physician.application | Capacidad profesional desacoplada de `users.role` (ADR-039): una cuenta existente (FREE/PREMIUM/PATIENT) puede solicitar ser profesional sin segunda cuenta ni pérdida de persona/plan/vertical. `PhysicianAccess.isPhysician(user)` es la única fuente de verdad (deriva de `physician_verification_status = APPROVED`); `JwtAuthenticationFilter` construye authorities por request (`ROLE_<persona>` + `ROLE_PHYSICIAN` si aplica) reutilizando el `User` en request-scope. Flujo: `POST/GET /health/physician/application` (solicitud PENDING, validación de email verificado y duplicados, sin tocar `role`/subscription) → decisión ADMIN en `/admin/users/physicians/{id}/approve|reject` (cualquier rol, con motivo, auditado con acciones PHYSICIAN_APPLICATION_*). `register/physician` mantiene 201 anti-enumeración y expone `state` (ACCOUNT_ALREADY_VERIFIED/ACCOUNT_NOT_VERIFIED/PHYSICIAN_PENDING/APPROVED/REJECTED/NEW_REGISTRATION); `resend-verification` expone `status` (SENT/ALREADY_VERIFIED/COOLDOWN/NO_ACCOUNT). `UserDTO`/`AuthResponse` exponen `physicianCapability`. Claves: PhysicianAccess, PhysicianApplicationService, AdminUserService, AuthServiceImpl.registerPhysician, JwtAuthenticationFilter, UserDTO/AuthResponse. |
